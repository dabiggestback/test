Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how School Dashboard ("we," "us," or "the Service") handles information when you use the School Dashboard website. We aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the Service and its optional features.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on which features you use, School Dashboard may process information necessary to provide the Service.

3. Google Account and Google Classroom Data

If you choose to sign in with Google or connect Google Classroom, Google may provide information necessary to authenticate you and provide the requested Classroom features.

This may include:

  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google Classroom courses
  • Classroom coursework and assignments
  • Assignment due dates
  • Information about your own Classroom submissions, where supported

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google Classroom is used only to provide School Dashboard features, including:

  • Displaying your classes
  • Displaying assignments and coursework
  • Displaying assignment due dates
  • Showing relevant Classroom information on your dashboard
  • Providing and maintaining the Google Classroom integration

We do not use Google Classroom information to create advertising profiles or for targeted advertising. We do not sell Google Classroom information.

5. Google User Data

School Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide the features requested by the user. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

6. Data Storage

School Dashboard is designed to minimize the amount of personal information stored. Google Classroom information is accessed only as necessary to provide the requested Classroom features.

If the Service stores information in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what is stored, why it is stored, and how long it is retained.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may use service providers that help operate School Dashboard, such as hosting and infrastructure providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of the Service or its users.

8. Google Account Access

Connecting Google Classroom is optional. You may revoke School Dashboard's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account settings.

Revoking access prevents School Dashboard from accessing Google Classroom information through the authorization you previously granted.

9. Student and Children's Privacy

School Dashboard may be used by students. We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide the Service and do not intentionally request unnecessary sensitive personal information from students.

Parents, guardians, schools, or users with questions about information handled by the Service may contact us using the information below.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Service. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Users should protect their Google accounts and should never share their passwords or authentication information with others.

11. Third-Party Services

School Dashboard may use third-party services, including Google services. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. School Dashboard is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

12. Your Choices

You may choose not to connect Google Classroom. If you have connected Google Classroom, you may revoke the application's Google access through your Google Account settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
